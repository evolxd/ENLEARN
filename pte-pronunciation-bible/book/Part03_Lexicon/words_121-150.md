# Words 121–150（词汇 121–150）

Part: Part III — PTE High Frequency Lexicon
Status: Draft (Batch 5 of the planned 300–500 word target; batch size 30 words, per `README.md`)
Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: same as Batches 1–4 — IPA and stress are standard, well-established dictionary pronunciations; this session's direct Cambridge/Oxford fetch attempts returned 403, so each entry should be individually cross-checked before being marked Done. "AI Recognition Notes" fields are `[Hypothesis]`-tagged reasoned extensions of Parts I and IV, not observed word-specific AI behavior data.

**Batch theme**: Essay/Summarize Written Text discourse connectors (*thus, despite, nevertheless, furthermore, moreover, regarding*) and cause-effect/action verbs (*argue, overcome, address, tackle, implement, execute, enhance, improve, facilitate, promote, encourage, discourage, prevent, avoid, reduce, minimize*) — the counterpart to Batch 4's Describe Image theme, oriented toward Part V Ch.8 (Essay).

**New/continuing patterns**: *avoid* is this lexicon's first example containing the /ɔɪ/ diphthong (the "choice" vowel, Part II Ch.3) — none of the prior 120 words used it. *encourage*/*discourage* show that adding a prefix does not shift primary stress off the root. *improve*/*improvement* and *consequence*/*consequently* are flagged as further non-shift examples (stress stays put across the word family), continuing the checks-and-balances started with *decline* in Batch 4. *address* is flagged for stress variability between its noun and verb senses rather than asserted as a clean stress pair like *record*/*present*, since dictionary sources disagree more here — a `[Hypothesis]`-tagged caution rather than a confident claim.

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable.
