# Words 421–450（词汇 421–450）

Part: Part III — PTE High Frequency Lexicon
Status: Draft (first half of Batch 11, a 60-word batch combined with `words_451-480.md` — see `README.md`)
Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: same as prior batches — IPA and stress are standard,
well-established dictionary pronunciations; this session's direct
Cambridge/Oxford fetch attempts returned 403, so each entry should be
individually cross-checked before being marked Done. "AI Recognition
Notes" fields are `[Hypothesis]`-tagged reasoned extensions of Parts I
and IV, not observed word-specific AI behavior data.

**Batch theme**: air travel and transportation logistics (*airport,
passenger, luggage, departure, arrival, boarding, terminal, customs,
immigration, passport, transit, delay, route, traffic, congestion*) and
weather/natural disasters (*weather, forecast, humidity, precipitation,
drought, flood, hurricane, thunderstorm, wind, rainfall, storm,
disaster, earthquake, wildfire, tornado*).

**New pattern — second explicit medial /ð/ example**: *weather* is
flagged as a dedicated medial-/ð/ target, joining *algorithm* (Batch 7)
as the second concrete data point for this phoneme in a non-initial
position (word-initial /ð/, as in *this*/*thus*, is covered in Part II
and IV directly).

**Continuing patterns**: this batch adds seven more compound-noun-stress
examples (*airport, passport, forecast, thunderstorm, rainfall,
earthquake, wildfire*), joining *network/software/hardware/database/
bandwidth* (Batch 8) and *breakthrough/household/childhood* (Batch 10).
*boarding* is a third velar-nasal /ŋ/ example, after *sibling* and
*upbringing* (Batch 10). *humidity* is a fifth "-ity" family data point.
*route* is flagged with a GA-vs-variant pronunciation caution, joining
*finance*/*financial*, *vaccine*, *advertisement*, *controversial*.

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner
Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable.
