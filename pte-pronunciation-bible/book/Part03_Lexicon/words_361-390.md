# Words 361–390（词汇 361–390）

Part: Part III — PTE High Frequency Lexicon
Status: Draft (first half of Batch 10, a 60-word batch combined with `words_391-420.md` — see `README.md`)
Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: same as prior batches — IPA and stress are standard,
well-established dictionary pronunciations; this session's direct
Cambridge/Oxford fetch attempts returned 403, so each entry should be
individually cross-checked before being marked Done. "AI Recognition
Notes" fields are `[Hypothesis]`-tagged reasoned extensions of Parts I
and IV, not observed word-specific AI behavior data.

**Batch theme**: scientific research methodology (*experiment,
laboratory, observe, observation, sample, survey, questionnaire, verify,
verification, replicate, empirical, quantitative, qualitative,
statistic, statistics, scientist, discovery, discover, invention,
invent, breakthrough, prototype, simulate, simulation, calculate,
calculation, measurement, measure, assess, assessment*).

**New pattern — four more "-tion" shift-from-base-word pairs**:
*observe*→*observation*, *verify*→*verification*, *simulate*→
*simulation*, *calculate*→*calculation* all move stress onto the
syllable before "-tion," extending the "-tion" shift-from-base-word
family started with *organize*→*organization* (Batch 6), *publish*→
*publication* (Batch 8), and *regulate*→*regulation* (Batch 9).

**New pattern — noun/verb stress-shift pair**: *survey* is stressed on
syllable 1 as a noun and syllable 2 as a verb, joining Part II Ch.4's
*record*/*present* and this lexicon's *increase*/*decrease*, *impact*,
*contrast* (Batches 2 and 4).

**New pattern — second and third /ʒ/ examples**: *measurement* and
*measure* both contain /ʒ/, the second and third data points for this
phoneme after *leisure* (Batch 9).

**Continuing patterns**: *questionnaire* is a French-origin final-stress
loanword, joining *entrepreneur* (Batch 8) and *souvenir* (Batch 9).
*quantitative*/*qualitative* both carry a final coda /v/ (joining
*cognitive*, *competitive*, *narrative*) and form a minimal-pair-style
companion to *quantity*/*quality* (Batch 7). *empirical* is a second
"-ical" family example, joining *historical* (Batch 7). *breakthrough*
is another compound-noun-stress example (joining *network*, *software*,
*bandwidth*, Batch 8).

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner
Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable.
