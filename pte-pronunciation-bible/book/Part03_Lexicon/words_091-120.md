# Words 091–120（词汇 091–120）

Part: Part III — PTE High Frequency Lexicon
Status: Draft (Batch 4 of the planned 300–500 word target; batch size 30 words, per `README.md`)
Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: same as Batches 1–3 — IPA and stress are standard, well-established dictionary pronunciations; this session's direct Cambridge/Oxford fetch attempts returned 403, so each entry should be individually cross-checked before being marked Done. "AI Recognition Notes" fields are `[Hypothesis]`-tagged reasoned extensions of Parts I and IV, not observed word-specific AI behavior data.

**Batch theme**: this batch clusters around data-description vocabulary (trend words: *dramatic, gradual, steady, rapid, decline, fluctuate*; comparison words: *proportion, percentage, majority, minority, average, overall, compare, contrast, similar, differ, equivalent, correspond, represent*) — directly relevant to Part V Ch.3 (Describe Image) and data-based Essay prompts.

**New pattern this batch — adjective/verb suffix-vowel alternation**: *appropriate* and *approximate* are both spelled identically across their adjective and verb forms but end differently: the adjective ends in a reduced /ət/, the verb in a full diphthong /eɪt/. This is a distinct mechanism from the noun/verb *stress-shift* pattern tracked since Batch 2 (*increase/decrease*, *impact*) — here the stress position does not move, but the final vowel changes with grammatical role. *Contrast* (this batch) is confirmed as a genuine stress-shift pair, while *decline* is flagged as a **non-example**: despite looking similar to *increase/decrease*, its noun and verb forms share the same stress pattern.

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable.
