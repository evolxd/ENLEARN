Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: IPA and stress transcriptions in this batch are drawn from standard, well-established dictionary pronunciations (this session's attempts to fetch Cambridge Dictionary pages directly returned 403, consistent with the fetch-tool limitation noted throughout this project — see Part I's sourcing note). These are common, non-controversial vocabulary items whose pronunciations are stable across major dictionaries, but each entry should still be individually cross-checked against Cambridge or Oxford Learner's Dictionaries before being marked Done, per the Quality Checklist (`PROJECT.md` §5). "AI Recognition Notes" fields are reasoned extensions of the mechanisms established in Part I (scoring architecture) and Part IV (error patterns), tagged `[Hypothesis]` — they are not observed, word-specific AI behavior data.

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable, using the format "Part IV Ch.X Mistake Y."
