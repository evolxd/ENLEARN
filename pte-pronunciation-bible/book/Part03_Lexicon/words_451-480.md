# Words 451–480（词汇 451–480）

Part: Part III — PTE High Frequency Lexicon
Status: Draft (second half of Batch 11, combined with `words_421-450.md` to form a 60-word batch — see `README.md`)
Accent model: General American (GA), consistent with Parts II and IV

**Sourcing note**: same as prior batches — IPA and stress are standard,
well-established dictionary pronunciations; this session's direct
Cambridge/Oxford fetch attempts returned 403, so each entry should be
individually cross-checked before being marked Done. "AI Recognition
Notes" fields are `[Hypothesis]`-tagged reasoned extensions of Parts I
and IV, not observed word-specific AI behavior data.

**Batch theme**: careers and workplace practices (*interview, resume,
application, promotion, recruit, recruitment, onboarding, teamwork,
freelance, remote, overtime, appraisal, feedback, mentor, internship*)
and computing/internet technology (*password, username, upload,
download, browser, website, encryption, cybersecurity, malware,
firewall, cloud, streaming, subscription, app, notification*).

**New pattern — heteronym caution**: *resume* covers two entirely
unrelated words that happen to share a spelling in casual English: the
French-origin noun "résumé" (a CV, final-syllable stress, a fourth
French-origin final-stress loanword after *entrepreneur*, *souvenir*,
*questionnaire*) and the native English verb "resume" (to continue,
syllable-2 stress). This is a distinct mechanism from ordinary
noun/verb stress-shift pairs like *survey* (Batch 10), which share a
single root meaning — here the two words are historically and
semantically unconnected.

**Continuing patterns**: this batch adds nine more compound-noun-stress
examples (*teamwork, freelance, overtime, feedback, password,
username, download, website, malware, firewall* — ten, in fact),
extending the thread from Batches 8, 10, and this batch's companion
file. *onboarding* and *streaming* are the fourth and fifth velar-nasal
/ŋ/ examples. *internship* is a third confirmation that "-ship" is
stress-neutral. *cybersecurity* is a sixth "-ity" data point, in this
lexicon's longest compound word.

Each entry follows `book/_TEMPLATE_LEXICON_ENTRY.md`. Chinese Learner
Errors cross-reference Part IV's numbered mistakes where applicable.
