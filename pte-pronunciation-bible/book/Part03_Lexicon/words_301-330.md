**Batch theme**: law/justice (*law, legal, legislation, justice, judge, court, crime, criminal, penalty, regulation, authority*) and politics/civics (*election, candidate, campaign, democracy, citizen, constitution, parliament, senate, diplomat*).

**New/continuing patterns**: *regulate*→*regulation* is a third concrete "-tion" shift-from-base-verb pair (after *organize*→*organization*, Batch 6, and *publish*→*publication*, Batch 8). *democracy*→*democratic* and *diplomat*→*diplomatic* extend the "-ic" rule's shift family (joining *economy*/*economic*, Batch 3). *citizen*→*citizenship* shows that "-ship" is stress-neutral, unlike every other suffix tracked so far — a new negative data point worth noting explicitly.
