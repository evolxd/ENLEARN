### Format and item types

The Reading section is commonly reported to last around 29–30 minutes with 13–18 questions across five item types `[Practice]` (secondary-sourced; re-verify before publication):

1. **Multiple Choice, Single Answer** — select the one correct option.
2. **Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers** — select all correct options (no partial credit is typically implied for this type in the sources found; treat with caution pending verification).
3. **Fill in the Blanks (Dropdown)** — select the best word for each blank from a dropdown list; partial credit, one point per correct blank `[Practice]`.
4. **Fill in the Blanks (Drag and Drop)** — drag words into blanks; also reported with partial, per-blank credit `[Practice]`, and is described as testing reading and writing together (grammar, collocation, vocabulary, context).
5. **Reorder Paragraphs** — arrange scrambled paragraphs into the original logical order, requiring identification of main ideas and logical/cohesive connections between paragraphs.

Fill in the Blanks (both types combined) is reported to make up the largest share of questions in this section, and to affect both the Reading and Writing communicative scores `[Practice]`.

## Examples

A Reorder Paragraphs task typically rewards identifying a paragraph with a clear topic sentence or introductory marker (a likely first paragraph) and looking for cohesive links — pronouns, transition words ("however", "as a result"), and topic continuity — between remaining paragraphs, rather than reading and re-reading the full set repeatedly without a structural strategy.

## Exam Strategy

- For Fill in the Blanks items, use collocation and grammar knowledge (Part III's "Related Words" and "Common Mistakes" fields) to narrow options before final selection.
- For Reorder Paragraphs, identify the likely opening paragraph first, then build outward using cohesion markers rather than attempting a single linear read-through guess.
- For Multiple Choice items, eliminate clearly incorrect options first rather than searching for the correct one directly.
- Manage time across the section deliberately, since it combines both quick-answer items (Multiple Choice) and more time-consuming items (Reorder Paragraphs, longer Fill in the Blanks passages).

## Exercises

1. Practice a Reorder Paragraphs set by first identifying the likely opening paragraph, then listing the cohesion markers that link each subsequent paragraph.
2. For a Fill in the Blanks (Dropdown) passage, identify which blanks you can resolve confidently via grammar/collocation alone, versus which require full-sentence comprehension.
3. Time yourself on a full Reading section practice set and note which item type consumed a disproportionate share of your time.

## Summary

- The Reading section covers five item types: Multiple Choice (single/multiple answer), Fill in the Blanks (dropdown/drag-and-drop), and Reorder Paragraphs.
- Fill in the Blanks item types are reported to carry partial, per-blank credit and to make up the largest share of the section.
